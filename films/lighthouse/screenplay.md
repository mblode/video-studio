# The Last Watch — silent screenplay

> THE LAST WATCH

## EXT. LIGHTHOUSE HEADLAND - DUSK - [s01, 0:03–0:11]

Wind flattens the grass around a dark lighthouse. In the cottage, THE KEEPER
closes a small suitcase. On the wet path below, THE RELIEF climbs toward him
with a tool roll and a compact motor case.

He sees the case before he sees her face.

SOUND: hard wind, distant surf, suitcase latches, boots on wet gravel.

## INT. TOWER ENTRANCE - DUSK - [s02, 0:11–0:19]

The relief offers her hand. The keeper takes her motor case instead, sets it
beside the door, and shuts the gale outside.

She reaches toward the dark iron service key on the table. His hand closes around
it first. He clips it to his belt and places two enamel mugs far apart.

SOUND: iron hinge, door slam, case on stone, metal chime, ceramic on wood.

## INT. MACHINERY ROOM - DUSK - [s03, 0:19–0:27]

The keeper winds the counterweight and listens to the clockwork.

The relief clamps a grey vibration meter to the drive. Its needle kicks. She
stops a worn chain link between gloved fingers and holds a fresh spare beside
it.

He compares them, returns the spare to her roll, and winds the weight higher.

SOUND: chain rising, clockwork tick, meter click, one strained metal knock.

## INT. RADIO ROOM / EXT. SEA - NIGHT - [s04, 0:27–0:35]

Static bursts from the radio as storm fog swallows the headland. The relief
wipes the window. A small work boat, engine steady, follows the last visible
water between reef and cliff.

The keeper looks from the boat to the dark lamp room and climbs.

SOUND: radio static, rain on glass, distant diesel, one low horn, iron stairs.

## INT. LAMP AND MACHINERY ROOMS - NIGHT - [s05, 0:35–0:43]

The keeper strikes the igniter. Amber light fills the Fresnel lens.

He drives the service key home and engages the clockwork. The relief points to
the meter's hammering needle. He keeps his hand on the lever.

The worn link bucks under load and parts inside its guard. The lens stops. Its
beam lies uselessly across inland rock.

SOUND: ignition thump, gears rising, chain strain, steel snap, alarm bell.

## EXT. WORK BOAT - STORM NIGHT - [s06, 0:43–0:51]

The boat's propeller churns and its rudder answers, but fog has erased the safe
channel. White reef water appears ahead.

The skipper turns away from one breaker and finds another. A handheld signal
flashes twice toward the dark lighthouse.

SOUND: working diesel, wheel chain, hull impact, two signal clicks, horn.

## INT. MACHINERY ROOM - STORM NIGHT - [s07, 0:51–0:59]

The keeper jams the service key into the manual hand-crank and heaves. The
broken weight train does not move.

The crank kicks back. The key cuts his left palm and rings across the floor.
The relief catches him before he strikes the stone.

The boat's horn reaches them through the wall.

SOUND: strained gears, metal kick, key on stone, cloth tearing, muffled horn.

## INT. TOWER ENTRANCE / MACHINERY ROOM - STORM NIGHT - [s08, 0:59–1:07]

The relief wraps his palm, then runs downstairs and retrieves the motor case
from where he shelved it.

She bolts the compact motor to the service shaft. It spins, but the lens remains
locked to the broken weight train. A dark iron clutch cover will not open.

She turns and holds out her hand toward the key on the keeper's belt.

SOUND: boots on iron stairs, case latches, bolts, motor whine, locked metal.

## INT. MACHINERY AND LAMP ROOMS - STORM NIGHT - [s09, 1:07–1:15]

Another horn. The keeper looks up at the frozen beam, then places the service
key in the relief's open hand.

He points out the concealed release. She unlocks the clutch while he holds the
broken train clear. She couples the motor.

The flywheel turns. Amber bars begin travelling across both faces.

SOUND: metal chime, clutch clack, motor rising, gears catching, glass resonance.

## EXT. WORK BOAT AND REEF - STORM NIGHT - [s10, 1:15–1:23]

The restored beam sweeps across the boat and reveals black open water beyond
the last white breaker.

The skipper turns the working wheel hand over hand. The bow follows. The reef
falls behind and the wake straightens into deep water.

SOUND: horn answering, wheel chain, engine climbing, surf receding.

## INT./EXT. TOWER ENTRANCE - DAWN - [s11, 1:23–1:31]

Two enamel mugs sit together beside the closed logbook. The keeper's bandaged
hand rests near the dark iron service key. He pushes the key across the table.

The relief closes her hand around it. He lifts his suitcase and walks down the
wet path. At the bend, he looks back once at the turning light.

SOUND: mugs on wood, key sliding, door hinge, gulls, boots on gravel.

## INT./EXT. LAMP ROOM - NEXT DUSK - [s12, 1:31–1:39]

A clean replacement link moves through the repaired drive. The relief checks
its rhythm with her grey meter, removes the probe, and winds with the iron key.

She stops and listens as the keeper did. Satisfied, she starts the lamp.

The key and meter share the foreground ledge as the beam sweeps toward a
distant unknown vessel.

SOUND: even clockwork, meter click, ignition thump, steady wind, gentle surf.

> THE WATCH PASSES ON

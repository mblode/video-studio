# The Last Watch — silent screenplay

## 1. Exterior, lighthouse headland, dusk

Wind drives the grass flat. In the cottage window, THE KEEPER closes a small
suitcase. He looks toward the dark tower. Far down the path, THE RELIEF walks
into the weather with a canvas tool roll.

## 2. Interior, tower entrance

The iron door shuts on the storm. The relief offers a hand. The keeper hangs her
wet coat, places two enamel mugs apart on the table, and keeps the brass winding
key when she reaches for the ring.

## 3. Interior, machinery room

The keeper winds the counterweight and listens to its tick. The relief tests the
drive with a grey meter. Its needle jumps as the weight shudders. He moves the
meter aside and closes the panel.

## 4. Interior, radio room / exterior sea

A warning lamp flashes over radio static. The barometer drops. Through rain on
the window, one small boat disappears into fog. Both keepers look up at the dark
lamp room.

## 5. Interior, lamp room

The keeper strikes the igniter. Amber fire fills the Fresnel lens. The clockwork
engages and the beam begins its circle. The relief watches the mechanism rather
than the light.

## 6. Interior, machinery room

Lightning turns the windows white. The drive chain snaps across the room. The
flywheel stops. Above, the beam freezes against the cliff.

## 7. Exterior / interior, boat at sea

The boat pitches toward white water. A figure at the wheel sees no beam and
fires a weak signal lamp. The reef appears through spray ahead.

## 8. Interior, machinery room

The keeper jams the brass key into the manual socket and heaves. The seized
clutch kicks back. He falls, his left palm cut. The relief catches him before he
strikes the stone floor.

## 9. Interior, machinery room

The relief acts. She opens the panel, traces the dead circuit, releases a hidden
clutch cover, and lays a compact service motor beside the flywheel. She looks to
the keeper. He looks from her motor to his key.

## 10. Interior / exterior, lamp room

He places the key in her hand. He holds the clutch open while she couples the
motor and brings it up to speed. The flywheel turns. Above them, amber light
starts moving across the glass.

## 11. Exterior, boat at sea

The beam finds the wheelhouse. The figure spins the wheel. The stern clears the
breaking reef and the wake straightens toward open water.

## 12. Interior / exterior, tower entrance, dawn

Rainwater drips from two mugs now sitting together. The keeper lays the brass
key beside the logbook. The relief takes it. He leaves with his suitcase. At the
bend he looks back once; she stands in the tower door.

## 13. Interior / exterior, lamp room, next dusk

The relief sets the grey meter beside the brass key. She winds the drive, stops,
and listens to its rhythm as he did. Satisfied, she starts the lamp. The beam
sweeps across the sea toward a distant unknown vessel.

## Cards

Opening: **THE LAST WATCH**

Closing: **THE WATCH PASSES ON**

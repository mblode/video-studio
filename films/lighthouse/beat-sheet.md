# The Last Watch — beat sheet

## Story spine

Once upon a time, a veteran keeper believed the light was safest in his hands.
Every night he performed the same exact ritual. Until one day, a young relief
arrived for his final watch. Because of that, he guarded the work instead of
teaching it. Because of that, the warning signs in the mechanism went unheard.
Because of that, the drive failed as a boat approached the reef. Until finally,
he trusted her method and she trusted his knowledge. Ever since then, the watch
continued in hands able to carry both.

## Escalation ladder

| Stage | Keeper’s gift | Relief’s gift | Relationship |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Setup | Knows the ritual and tower by touch | Notices measurable anomalies | He excludes; she observes |
| Pressure | Lights the lamp from memory | Sees the counterweight shudder | He dismisses the warning |
| Failure | Identifies the seized clutch | Traces the broken drive path | Both methods are incomplete |
| Combination | Holds the manual clutch open | Couples the service motor safely | First shared frame and rhythm |
| Legacy | Leaves the key | Keeps key and meter together | Stewardship replaces ownership |

## Repetition with change

- s01 and s13 share the same dusk geometry. First, the lamp is dark and the
  keeper faces the tower alone; last, the lamp turns and the relief faces the
  sea.
- s05 and s10 both ignite motion. The first is a solitary ritual; the second is
  coordinated work.
- s04, s07, and s11 track the boat as glimpse, danger, then corrected course.
- s02 and s12 use the same table. The key and mugs move from separation to
  shared space.

## Continuity and anchor map

Every generation uses an independent `first_frame` still with the same id and
seed. No shot chains from another. This costs more keyframes but keeps all 13
shots parallel, prevents drift, and makes any retake local.

The stills must preserve:

- keeper and relief wardrobe and apparent age
- key ownership by shot
- injured left palm from s08 through s12
- weather progression from dusk to storm to dawn to next dusk
- dark / turning / frozen / restored lamp states
- boat heading toward reef in s07 and away in s11

## Theme audit

The characters never say what the film means. The final image does the work:
old key and new meter occupy the same ledge while the inherited beam reaches a
stranger at sea.

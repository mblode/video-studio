# The Last Watch — beat sheet

## Story spine

Once upon a time, a veteran keeper maintained a lighthouse by sound, touch, and
ritual. Every night he alone wound the drive and kept its service key. But one
day, his replacement arrived for his final watch with inspection tools and an
emergency motor. Because of that, he treated the handover as a challenge and
ignored the worn chain link she found. Because of that, the link broke under
the load of his last lighting while a boat approached hidden reef water.
Because of that, his hand-crank failed and her motor remained blocked by the
clutch only his key could release. Until finally, he chose the duty over his
pride, passed her the key, and combined his knowledge with her tool. Ever since
then, the watch has continued with both.

## Causality audit

- s01 **but** the keeper sees his replacement arrive.
- s02 **therefore** he asserts ownership by shelving her motor and retaining the
  key.
- s03 **therefore** he rejects her evidence and runs the worn drive.
- s04 **meanwhile** storm fog makes the lighthouse necessary to an approaching
  boat.
- s05 **because of his refusal**, the loaded link breaks and the beam stops.
- s06 **therefore** the still-controllable boat loses the safe visual channel.
- s07 **therefore** he tries the only method he trusts and fails.
- s08 **therefore** she brings the prepared alternative, but it needs his
  protected access and knowledge.
- s09 **therefore** he must choose; trust restores the turning light.
- s10 **therefore** the skipper sees safe water and steers clear.
- s11–s12 **therefore** the keeper can leave and the changed watch continues.

## Theme audit

The theme is never spoken. The keeper's key first blocks access, then enables a
shared repair, then rests beside the relief's meter while the inherited beam
reaches a stranger.

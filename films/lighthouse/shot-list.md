# The Last Watch — shot list

One entry is one eight-second generation. Every entry opens on a dedicated
2200×2000 still as `first_frame`; every shot requests adaptive ratio to preserve
the literal 1.10:1 composition. If the provider returns square, the assembled
delivery is centre-conformed to exact 11:10. The whole film is stark
orthochromatic black-and-white with hard practical light, crushed maritime
blacks, silver halation, coarse grain, and slight gate weave.

## s01 · `s01-last-arrival` · 8s · KEYFRAME

- **Frame:** almost-square exterior wide, white tower rising through the top of
  frame, cottage window below, headland path entering at the bottom
- **Action:** keeper closes suitcase; relief climbs carrying tool roll and a
  distinct dark motor case; he notices the case before her face
- **Camera:** exterior push, cottage hand insert, compressed path close
- **Audio:** hard wind, surf, suitcase latches, boots on wet gravel
- **Continuity:** establishes suitcase, motor case, dark lamp, final watch

## s02 · `s02-refusal` · 8s · KEYFRAME

- **Frame:** cramped entrance two-shot divided vertically by the open iron door
- **Action:** relief offers hand; keeper takes and shelves motor case; she
  reaches for service key; he clips it to his belt and separates two mugs
- **Camera:** close wide-angle track, case insert, locked table two-shot
- **Audio:** hinge, door slam, case on stone, metal chime, ceramic on wood
- **Continuity:** motor remains by entrance through s07; key remains with keeper;
  mugs are visibly apart

## s03 · `s03-worn-link` · 8s · KEYFRAME

- **Frame:** vertical weight chain divides the pair in the machinery room
- **Action:** keeper winds and listens; relief clamps meter and stops a worn
  link; she compares fresh spare; he puts spare back and winds higher
- **Camera:** low vertical track, macro link comparison, locked confrontation
- **Audio:** chain lift, clockwork tick, meter click, strained metal knock
- **Continuity:** intact worn link is clearly established; no injury; his
  decision, not weather, creates the risk

## s04 · `s04-boat-approaches` · 8s · KEYFRAME

- **Frame:** radio-room window above the pair, fog swallowing sea outside
- **Action:** static bursts; relief wipes glass; working boat follows the last
  visible channel; keeper looks from boat to lamp and climbs
- **Camera:** radio close, fast pan to window, long-lens boat geography
- **Audio:** radio static, rain on glass, steady diesel, low horn, iron steps
- **Continuity:** boat engine and steering work; reef and safe channel are
  visible before fog closes; lamp remains dark

## s05 · `s05-pride-breaks-drive` · 8s · KEYFRAME

- **Frame:** keeper fills foreground at the dark Fresnel lens; relief and meter
  are trapped behind the vertical lens frame
- **Action:** lamp ignites; keeper engages drive despite meter hammering; worn
  link parts inside guard; beam stops across inland rock
- **Camera:** hard-lit close wide, macro link failure, exterior tower profile
- **Audio:** igniter, flame, gears rising, chain strain and snap, alarm bell
- **Continuity:** explicit cause-and-effect payoff of s03; lightning may flash
  outside but does not strike or cause failure

## s06 · `s06-reef-in-dark` · 8s · KEYFRAME

- **Frame:** sea-level almost-square triangle of boat below, white reef above
  right, black open water obscured left
- **Action:** propeller churns; rudder answers; skipper turns around one breaker
  into another because the channel is invisible; signal flashes twice
- **Camera:** hull-level wide, wheel-and-rudder matched insert, stern compression
- **Audio:** working diesel, wheel chain, hull impact, signal clicks, horn
- **Continuity:** no steering or engine failure; danger is navigation in fog

## s07 · `s07-hand-crank-fails` · 8s · KEYFRAME

- **Frame:** low cramped machinery-room frame, keeper at hand-crank, relief
  behind, broken guarded chain overhead
- **Action:** he jams in service key and heaves; jammed train kicks back; key
  cuts left palm and drops; relief catches him; boat horn reaches room
- **Camera:** shoulder push, hand macro, low two-shot
- **Audio:** gear strain, metal kick, key on stone, cloth tear, muffled horn
- **Continuity:** left palm injury begins; motor remains downstairs; key returns
  to keeper after bandaging

## s08 · `s08-motor-needs-key` · 8s · KEYFRAME

- **Frame:** relief hauls the established motor case up the spiral stair while
  bandaged keeper remains above
- **Action:** she bolts motor to shaft; motor spins but lens stays locked; iron
  clutch cover refuses to open; she holds out her hand toward his belt key
- **Camera:** stairwell tilt, overhead coupling plan, locked eyeline close
- **Audio:** boots on iron, case latches, bolts, motor whine, locked metal
- **Continuity:** pays off s01–s02 motor setup; shows why her tool alone cannot
  solve the problem

## s09 · `s09-trust-turns-light` · 8s · KEYFRAME

- **Frame:** close two-shot over exposed service shaft, relief's open palm
  between them, keeper holding key, motor already mounted
- **Action:** second horn; keeper places key in her palm; he points out release;
  she unlocks clutch while he clears broken train; she couples motor; lens turns
- **Camera:** locked hand close, low mechanical two-shot, crane into lamp room
- **Audio:** horn, metal chime, clutch clack, motor rise, glass resonance
- **Continuity:** protagonist's choice and mechanical climax occur together; key
  ownership changes visibly

## s10 · `s10-safe-water` · 8s · KEYFRAME

- **Frame:** high almost-square sea wide, restored beam joining tower, boat, and
  black open water while reef breaks on the opposite side
- **Action:** beam reveals safe channel; skipper turns working wheel; bow
  follows; wake straightens beyond last breaker
- **Camera:** descending wide, wheel close, stern pullback
- **Audio:** answering horn, wheel chain, engine climbing, surf receding
- **Continuity:** mirror s04 and s06 geography; light supplies information, not
  propulsion or steering

## s11 · `s11-duty-handed-over` · 8s · KEYFRAME

- **Frame:** silver-dawn table close, two mugs together, bandaged hand and
  service key beside a blank closed logbook
- **Action:** keeper pushes key across; relief takes it; he leaves with suitcase;
  looks back once at turning light
- **Camera:** locked still-life, doorway medium, long exterior
- **Audio:** mugs, key sliding, hinge, gulls, boots on gravel
- **Continuity:** internal payoff; no readable logbook text; palm wrapped

## s12 · `s12-next-watch` · 8s · KEYFRAME

- **Frame:** repaired drive in foreground with clean link, relief beyond, key and
  meter sharing a narrow ledge in the windowless machinery room
- **Action:** she checks meter; winds with key; stops to listen; starts lamp;
  beam reaches a distant vessel
- **Camera:** macro repaired link, cut upstairs to hard-lit lamp-room profile,
  exterior pullback
- **Audio:** even clockwork, meter click, ignition, steady wind, gentle surf
- **Continuity:** final image; combines her practice with his knowledge without
  copying his ritual unchanged

## Paid setup audit

- Motor case: visible s01, shelved s02, retrieved s08, coupled s09.
- Worn link: discovered and compared with spare s03, breaks under chosen load
  s05, appears repaired s12.
- Boat: enters with working engine and steering s04, loses only navigation s06,
  turns under its own power s10.
- Key: withheld s02, used unsuccessfully s07, required by both methods s08,
  passed during climax s09, inherited s11–s12.

## Dependency audit

All 12 shots are independent keyframes with new IDs and seeds. The obsolete
first draft remains immutable under its old IDs and cannot be selected for this
revision by mistake.

## Edit audit

- s01–s04: 0.35–0.45 second transitions for setup and readable geography
- s05–s09: 0.1–0.25 second transitions through crisis
- s10: 0.4 second release into safety
- s11–s12: 0.6–0.8 second transitions into the changed ritual
- Opening card is fully visible on frame zero
- Closing card follows the final proof image rather than replacing it
